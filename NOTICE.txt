This is a clean snapshot with fresh Git history. Excluded from it: credentials and .env
files, results databases, run outputs, evidence archives, recordings, backups, the
original research/pilot datasets and gold answers, private Git history and personal data.

The bundled suites (suites/*.yaml) and preview fixtures are SYNTHETIC smoke fixtures.
They validate the software only. They are not benchmark results and evaluate no AI model.
